Finnish Cancer Registry, new cancer cases (ilmaantuvuus) by welfare area, sex, 2020-2024 (five-year total, not per year).
Source: https://tilastot.syoparekisteri.fi/syovat/ (menu: Uudet syöpätapaukset; Viimeisin 5-vuotisjakso 2020-2024).
Sites: all cancers (C00-96, D09.0-1, D32-33, D41-43, D45-47, D76) and lung/trachea (C33-34).
Rates: age-standardised per 100,000, Finland 2014 standard. Pulled 2026-11-14 by reading the app's table (its CSV button returned HTML).
Helsinki city is separate from the four Uusimaa areas. Åland not pulled. Sexes are separate: no combined rate was pulled.
