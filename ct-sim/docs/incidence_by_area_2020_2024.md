# Cancer incidence by welfare area (Finnish Cancer Registry, 2020-2024) next to deaths and oncology referrals

Annual new cases = five-year total / 5 (all sexes). National: 38,324 a year; sum of areas: 38,066 (the gap is mostly Åland and unassigned residence; not checked). Deaths: Statistics Finland, all cancers, 2023-25 average. Rates are age-standardised per 100,000 (Finland 2014 standard) for men (M) and women (F); lung incidence = C33-34 (registry); lung deaths = Statistics Finland cause group 11, which is C32-34 (includes larynx), so lung deaths per case is slightly overstated. HUS rates are population-weighted averages of its five areas (approximation).
