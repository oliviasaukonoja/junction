About the glasses & prototype

Requires Meta Ray-Ban Display for lens visuals. Ordinary Ray-Ban Meta and Oakley Meta can play the guide audio; the 3D preview stays on the phone.

Circling and jumps move inside the lens overlay, with simulated depth. The app does not track your room or place a spider behind your actual body.

Use while sitting or standing still in a clear space. This is an experimental practice tool; clinical effectiveness has not been established.

App v0.5.0 · Android 12+ · ARM64. Audio and STOP tests are optional. Every presentation starts only from your Start button.
